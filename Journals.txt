TIER 1 – TOP PRESTIGE (NO APC FOR SUBSCRIPTION / OPEN ACCESS OPTIONS)

1.  Nature
    - Publisher: Springer Nature
    - APC: None for traditional subscription (Open Access optional, cost ~€10,850)
    - Impact Factor: ~64.8
    - Acceptance Rate: ~8%
    - Notes: Subscribe-to-Open agreements may allow free OA for some authors.
    - URL: https://www.nature.com

2.  Science
    - Publisher: AAAS
    - APC: None (author may share final accepted version immediately)
    - Impact Factor: ~44.7
    - Acceptance Rate: ~7%
    - Notes: Science allows immediate public access without fee via its public access policy.
    - URL: https://www.science.org

3.  Cell
    - Publisher: Cell Press / Elsevier
    - APC: None for subscription; Open Access optional (~$5,200)
    - Impact Factor: ~41.6
    - Acceptance Rate: <10%
    - URL: https://www.cell.com/cell/home

4.  Nature Biotechnology
    - Publisher: Springer Nature
    - APC: None for traditional subscription (OA optional)
    - Impact Factor: ~46.9
    - Acceptance Rate: ~10%
    - URL: https://www.nature.com/nbt/

5.  Nature Methods
    - Publisher: Springer Nature
    - APC: None for subscription (OA optional)
    - Impact Factor: ~36.8
    - Acceptance Rate: ~12%
    - URL: https://www.nature.com/nmeth/

6.  Genome Research
    - Publisher: Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Press

7.  Genome Biology

8.  eLife

================================================================================
RECOMMENDED FREE OPTIONS FOR TMO MANUSCRIPT
================================================================================

For a no‑fee submission, your best bets are subscription‑based hybrid journals
where you can choose not to pay APC by not opting for open access. However,
you must check each journal’s policy: some require OA for all articles.

Top choices (no APC for subscription):

1. Nature
2. Science
3. Cell
4. Nature Biotechnology
5. Nature Methods
6. Genome Research
7. Bioinformatics
8. Briefings in Bioinformatics
9. Nucleic Acids Research
10. Computational Biology and Chemistry

All of these journals are indexed in Web of Science / Scopus, have high impact
factors, and allow traditional (non‑OA) publication at no charge. Acceptance
rates are low (7‑30%), but your work is strong enough to submit.

================================================================================
ALTERNATIVE: DIAMOND OPEN ACCESS JOURNALS (NO APC FOR ANYONE)
================================================================================

Some truly no‑fee open access journals exist, but they are rare in computational
biology. Examples include:

- Journal of Statistical Distributions and Applications (Springer – check)
- Journal of Open Source Software (JOSS – for software papers)
- Peer Community In (PCI) recommendations (not a journal, but a validation)

None of these are ideal for your methods paper.

================================================================================
FINAL ADVICE
================================================================================

- For the highest impact, target **Nature Methods** or **Nature Biotechnology**.
- For a more balanced approach, try **Bioinformatics** or **Genome Research**.
- If you want to publish open access without fee, check if your institution has
  a transformative agreement covering APCs (most major universities do).

Check the journal’s specific policy before submission.
